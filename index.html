Quickstart

New here? This is the fast way into either app.

Scribble Reel

  1. Open Scribble Reel — no sign-up, it loads straight into a blank canvas.
  2. Draw your first frame with the pen tool, then hit add frame to start the next one.
  3. Turn on onion skin so you can see the previous frame while drawing the next.
  4. Hit play to preview the loop, then export as a GIF, MP4, WebM or PNG sequence.

Brush Maker

  1. Open Brush Maker and pick a starting shape for your brush tip — or import an existing .abr (Photoshop), .gbr / .gih (GIMP/Krita) brush file to start from.
  2. Adjust size, spacing, jitter and texture, and watch the live stroke preview update.
  3. Name your brush, then export it as JSON to save it to your device.
  4. In Scribble Reel, open the brush picker and use Import brush to load the file in — it also accepts Photoshop .abr and GIMP/Krita .gbr / .gih brush files if you've got those.
It all runs on your own device. Everything happens in your browser. Your drawings, projects and brushes are saved on your machine and are never uploaded anywhere.
