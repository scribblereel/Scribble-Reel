Resolution

Resize canvas keeps every existing pixel exactly where it is — nothing is stretched. Growing it adds transparent space around your art; shrinking it crops away anything that falls outside the new area. Resize project instead stretches or squishes the whole drawing to fill the new size — nothing is cropped, but proportions can change.
